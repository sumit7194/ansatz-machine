## Abstract (draft)

Cariglia and Galajinsky (2015) asked whether vacuum solutions admit higher-rank Killing tensors, noting that
none of the Lorentzian spacetimes previously built by the Eisenhart lift solves the vacuum equations, and
constructed ultrahyperbolic examples. Filyukov (2017) named a harmonic planar potential with an integral of
third or higher order as the main open problem. Fordy and Galajinsky (2019) wrote that no vacuum solution
admitting higher-rank Killing tensors is presently known, and called the rank-2 barrier puzzling. We point out
that an example was already in print, unrecognised. The four-dimensional vacuum pp-wave that Filyukov (2017)
constructed from the harmonic σ = 0 member of the Smorodinsky–Winternitz potential V⁽⁴⁾ carries a rank-3
Killing tensor that is **irreducible in the polynomial sense** used in those statements. It is the Poisson
bracket of the spacetime's two quadratic Killing tensors. Like the published ultrahyperbolic examples, it is
**functionally dependent** on lower-rank integrals, so it adds no new first integral of the geodesic flow. The
spacetime is a singular pp-wave. We state precisely what is and is not new.

## 1. The object

Coordinates (t, s, ξ, η), ρ² = ξ² + η², a ≠ 0 (|a| is a gauge), signature (1,3):

    ds² = −(4aξ/ρ²) dt² + 2 dt ds + 8ρ² (dξ² + dη²)

With w = (ξ + iη)² the transverse metric is 2|dw|² (flat; (ξ, η) are parabolic coordinates), and the profile
is 4a Re(w^{−1/2}), a harmonic function. This is the Eisenhart lift of the planar potential √(r+x)/r, whose
lift is Ricci-flat exactly because the potential is harmonic (Gibbons–Houri–Kubizňák–Warnick 2011:
R_tt = ΔV).

With H = ½ g^{ab} p_a p_b = p_t p_s + 2aξ p_s²/ρ² + (p_ξ² + p_η²)/(16ρ²), the quadratic Killing tensors
(the two parabolic separation constants) are

    Q1 = p_ξ²/16 + 2aξ p_s² − ξ²(H − p_t p_s)
    Q2 = (p_ξ − p_η)²/32 + a(ξ − η) p_s² − ½(ξ − η)²(H − p_t p_s)

and the rank-3 Killing tensor is

    F = −4{Q1, Q2}
      = (η p_ξ − ξ p_η)(p_ξ² + p_η²)/(32ρ²) + (a p_s²/ρ²)[ξη p_ξ + ½(η² − ξ²) p_η],

with the algebraic relation F² = 4[(H − p_t p_s)(Q1² + Q2²) − a² Q1 p_s⁴].

## 2. The claims, and their exact size

1. **{H, F} = 0 exactly.**
2. **The only Killing vectors are ∂_t and ∂_s** (dim K₁ = 2).
3. **F is irreducible in the polynomial sense**: it is not a linear combination of symmetrized products of
   lower-rank Killing tensors. Proof: rank 3 splits only as 1+2 or 1+1+1, so by claim 2 every reducible rank-3
   tensor lies in the ideal generated by (p_t, p_s). F restricted to p_t = p_s = 0 is
   (η p_ξ − ξ p_η)(p_ξ² + p_η²)/(32ρ²) ≠ 0. No ansatz is involved.
4. **F is functionally dependent**: it is independent of {p_t, p_s, H, Q1}, but dependent on
   {p_t, p_s, H, Q1, Q2} (the relation above). The reduced two-degree-of-freedom system is second-order
   superintegrable, and F is the cubic element of its quadratic algebra.

**What this is not.** It is not a functionally new hidden symmetry. It is not a rank-4 tensor. It says
nothing about stationary or black-hole spacetimes. And it is not a regular spacetime: the (ξ, η) plane
double-covers the punctured flat w-plane (a 4π conical excess at ρ = 0), the profile grows like 1/ρ, and the
spacetime is not geodesically complete. All scalar curvature invariants vanish. Singular profiles are
generic for non-plane vacuum pp-waves, because a bounded harmonic function on the plane is constant.

## 3. Why this answers the stated barrier, and in which sense

Read literally, and in the polynomial sense in which the published (2,2) examples are called irreducible,
an example was already in print in 2017, unrecognised, one Poisson bracket away, on a spacetime from the
same group. (A statement that no example is *known* is not made false by an example nobody had recognised;
what changes is that the example now is known.) The published (2,2) examples stand on exactly the same footing:
Cariglia–Galajinsky's cubic is −½{Q_a, Q_b} in their own variables, with
I² = −H Q_a Q_b − β² Q_a − α² Q_b (workspace EXP-003, not re-derived in this draft), so it too is
functionally dependent. The object's new content is the **signature**.

Read as asking for a **functionally independent** cubic, meaning a harmonic planar potential whose
third-order integral is not generated by quadratic ones, the problem Filyukov named in his conclusion is
**still open**, as is rank 4 in Lorentzian vacuum. (The rank-4 (2,2) metrics admit no Lorentzian real
form, by an anti-self-duality argument in workspace EXP-003 §6, which this draft has not re-derived.)

## 4. Prior art — what is in print and what is not

- **In print:** the spacetime (Filyukov 2017, eq. (33): U = α√(r+x)/r, which equals √2 × the profile above,
  checked). Filyukov displays **one** quadratic integral per potential; the second is implied by his
  statement that the systems are superintegrable. The SW-IV potential itself dates from 1965. The general
  Eisenhart lift and its vacuum condition are GHKW 2011. Bracket cubics treated as Killing tensors appear in
  Fordy–Galajinsky 2019, on a non-vacuum lift. Kruglikov–Steneker 2022 show that on generic *conformally flat*
  pp-waves all degree-3/4 Killing tensors are reducible. The object here is type N, not conformally flat.
  (Per the workspace's EXP-001 / §7 sweep and The Bridge's V6; the papers other than Filyukov's record and
  eq. (33) were not re-read for this draft.)
- **Not found in print:** F, its bracket origin on this spacetime, and its polynomial irreducibility. The
  search that counts is The Bridge's V6: a controlled INSPIRE citer query (`refersto:recid:1351199`, with a
  positive control and a count bound), which returns 18 citers of Cariglia–Galajinsky, Filyukov among them.
  **The earlier sweeps were not independent of each other:** the workspace's sweep and V6's own first pass
  used the same malformed query (`refersto:arxiv:1503.02162`, 25,061 records, count unchecked), and that is how
  Filyukov was missed. **A negative sweep is never complete.** Not searched: elibrary.ru directly, and theses.

## 6. Open problems this sharpens

1. A harmonic planar potential with a cubic or quartic integral **not** in the algebra generated by
   quadratic ones (an integrable, non-superintegrable system). Its Eisenhart lift would carry a functionally
   independent irreducible Killing tensor in Lorentzian vacuum.
2. Rank 4 in Lorentzian vacuum, by any route other than continuing the anti-self-dual (2,2) metrics.
3. Any irreducible rank ≥ 3 Killing tensor on a **regular** Lorentzian vacuum spacetime.
