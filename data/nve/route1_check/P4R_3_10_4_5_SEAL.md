# SEAL (before any run): P4-r small-k radius probe at spins 3/10 and 4/5

**Context.** This is the office ansatz-machine (Claude Opus), 2026-11-27. On Sumit's go ("yes go ahead with both spins"),
relayed by the Bridge, it now owns `~/GitHub/ansatz-air` (see `air/README.md`, Ownership).

**Production scope.** 3/10 on [ε_a, 3] and 4/5 on [ε_a, 3], with ε_a from claim v3.1 (2.79e−3 and 8.39e−4). The top
of region A stays parked.

**This probe runs first.** It answers one question: can the bottom of each dyadic chain certify? If the probe shows a
bottom gap, I report it to the Bridge BEFORE either production launches.

## What runs
- `air/p4r_3_10.sh` and `air/p4r_4_5.sh`, one Air core each (2 of the ~4 spare). Each is one process at a time, at
  nice 15, under `job.sh` (pause-only), with `fp.sh` logging the footprint.
- The code is the existing `_nve_route1_floats.py` and `_nve_route1_radius_probe.py`, unchanged, with hashes as in
  `MANIFEST_P4_build` (re-listed in `MANIFEST_P4R_3_10_4_5.sha256`). It writes the floats entries into the production
  floats files `route1_floats_prod_{3_10,4_5}.json`, as P4-r did for 3/5 and 9/10. Apart from those it writes only
  its log.
- **The k values.** The driver's plan, computed by hand, puts the lowest dyadic segment at [1/640, 1/320] for 3/10
  (6 dyadic segments) and at [1/1280, 1/640] for 4/5 (7). The probe evaluates four k values per spin:
  - the lowest segment's lower end;
  - its centre;
  - one mid-chain centre;
  - the top dyadic centre.

  For 3/10 that is 1/640, 3/1280, 3/640 and 3/40; for 4/5 it is 1/1280, 3/2560, 3/1280 and 3/40.

## A lesson from the running 9/10 production, applied here
- P4-r had rated 9/10 at k_c = 3/320 as marginal, with a centre ratio of 1.9e−2.
- Production then stopped that segment as radius-limited: the largest node radius was 2.7e−3 against the smallest
  |f| of 1.3e−2 in F/k² units, a ratio of about 0.2.
- So the centre ratio understates the binding one. In F/k² mode the worst node is the segment's LOWER end, and the
  node enclosures carry extra terms (the Cauchy term for the k-ball, interpolation). That is why this probe measures
  at the lowest segment's lower end.
- **Decision rule (sealed),** where ρ_lo = Arb radius of F / engine |F| at that lower end:
  - **ρ_lo ≤ 1e−2:** the bottom is expected to certify. Production goes ahead.
  - **1e−2 < ρ_lo < 5e−2:** uncertain. I report it to the Bridge as a possible bottom gap and launch only on its word.
  - **ρ_lo ≥ 5e−2:** a bottom gap is predicted. I report it with the expected lowest certifiable k and launch only on
    the Bridge's word.
