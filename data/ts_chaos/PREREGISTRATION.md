2. **Finite-time Lyapunov exponent** from a shadow orbit with renormalisation. The verdict rests on the SLOPE of log
   FTLE versus log t: ≈ −1 is regular, a plateau (slope ≳ −0.3) is chaotic. Kerr must show ≈ −1 at the same t_max.
3. **Sticky-layer signatures:** finite bounded lifetime (escape or plunge after many crossings) where neighbours are
   eternal; and the box dimension of the section.
4. **Integrator honesty:** mass-shell (H) drift recorded per orbit. Every orbit called chaotic is re-run at a 10× tighter
   tolerance (adaptive DOP853, or a step 10× smaller). A verdict that changes with tolerance is VOID. Orbits that come
   within a set distance of the ring or of x = 1 are flagged.
5. A detection counts only if (1) and (2) both fire, at both tolerances, on an orbit that is bound for at least the
   analysis window. An independent reproduction by the Bridge's own integrator is offered before any claim.

   - **New FTLE criterion: S_ex ≥ 10** (e^10 of excess stretching).
