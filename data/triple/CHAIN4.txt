The Kerr rank-2 Killing tensor that K1_A corrects. NOT the textbook Carter constant:
at chi^0 it is -8*L^2 + P_phi^2. Coordinates x=r, y=cos(theta); chi = spin a; M=1.
The conserved object is CHAIN4 + eps*K1, so in terms of Carter Q (= L^2 at chi^0),
and since P_phi^2 is exactly conserved for any axisymmetric metric, that is Q - eps*K1/8.
